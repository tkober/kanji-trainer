Import

The import reads your WaniKani progress and takes it over. Everything at or above the chosen SRS stage comes in as known and never appears in the review queue — the thing WaniKani does not offer. Everything below keeps its stage and its WaniKani due date. Locked items become lessons.

Without the checkbox, anything you have worked on here is left alone — so a second import only picks up new WaniKani content. With it, the mapping is redrawn from scratch; that is how you correct a threshold that felt wrong once you started reviewing.

Study behaviour

Second chance on mistakes

An answer that would be wrong is held back once: Enter submits it anyway, Esc hands the field back for editing. Otherwise a typo costs exactly as much as not knowing the item — and below four characters there is no typo tolerance to catch it. The warning never reveals the right answer.

A batch is read in one go and quizzed in one go; 5 is WaniKani's default and about as much as fits between reading a mnemonic and being asked to produce it.

Random and mixed are the defaults on purpose: a fixed order lets the previous item cue the next one, so recall is never tested in isolation. Ties (common after an import, when many items share a due time) are always broken randomly, whichever order is chosen.

The default is 0,4,8,24,48,168,336,720,2880,0 — the same spacing as WaniKani. Stages 0 and 9 are 0 because nothing is scheduled there.
